Delete your Unbreakable Lock account

Two ways to do it. Both delete the same thing.

1. In the app — instant

If you still have Unbreakable Lock installed, this is the fastest route and takes effect immediately:

  1. Open Unbreakable Lock.
  2. Tap the account button, or go to Account.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm.

2. By email — if you have uninstalled the app

Send a deletion request from the email address you signed in with, so we can confirm the account is yours.

Email a deletion request

Requests are actioned within 30 days, normally much sooner.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account (email address, and name and profile picture if you used Google Sign-In) Permanently deleted
Your blocked app list, schedules and daily limits Deleted from the device when you delete in the app. This data only ever existed on your phone — it was never uploaded, so there is no copy elsewhere to delete.
App usage figures Never left your device. Removed when you uninstall the app.
Subscription and purchase records Held by Google Play and RevenueCat as a record of the transaction. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — see below.

Two things worth knowing first

  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play, not by us. Cancel it in the Play Store under Subscriptions, or you will continue to be charged.
  • Deletion is permanent. There is no recovery, and a Pro entitlement tied to that account may not transfer to a new one. If you only want to stop using the app, uninstalling is enough.

Retention

Once deleted, the account record is removed from Firebase Authentication and is not retained. Google Play may retain transaction records independently of us, where required for tax and accounting.
